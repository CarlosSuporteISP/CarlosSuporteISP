## 🚀 Executive snapshot

I am a **Senior Network & Infrastructure Engineer with 12+ years of experience** designing, deploying and operating ISP and enterprise environments. My strongest areas are **BGP, MPLS, OSPF, IPv4/IPv6, BNG, routing security, Linux/Debian, Docker, Proxmox and automation**.

My experience spans ISP backbone and aggregation, subscriber access, peering and IP transit, network services, observability, virtualization and high-availability infrastructure, with hands-on multi-vendor experience in **Huawei, MikroTik, Juniper, Cisco, Datacom, ZTE and Ubiquiti**.

Today I combine engineering consulting, Tier-3 troubleshooting, technical documentation, open-source projects and practical training for networking and infrastructure professionals.
